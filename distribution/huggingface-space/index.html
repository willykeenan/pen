KE Pen lets you draw over any desktop app, shares only the marked crop with your configured AI host, and keeps the ink visible until the AI explicitly says it understood.

Choose your download Inspect the source
1 · Mark locally

The native overlay captures a padded crop around the stroke. No account, cloud backend, telemetry, or listening port.

2 · Read without clearing

pen_read returns the crop and bounded metadata. The mark stays visible while the AI reasons.

3 · Complete explicitly

pen_complete acknowledges understanding and schedules the fade. Visual context never grants action authority.

The whole Pen is free. KE Pen 0.5.0 is completely free and open source, with no limited edition, paid tier, account, or feature gate. Downloads are available for universal macOS, Windows x64, and Linux x64. The current builds are unsigned; Linux requires X11 or XWayland. The installed app can copy a ready-made MCP setup that runs its embedded server without a separate Node.js install. This Space is documentation; screen images stay on the user's computer unless their configured AI host sends the MCP result to its provider.
